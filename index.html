The Exposure Audit

Contextual Barriers

Accidentally testing for privilege rather than actual capability.

Manager: Steve Goal: Tech Logic

"Explain how to program a multi-zone smart thermostat."

Click to reveal the trap

The Trap: Wealth & Exposure

Steve wants troubleshooting logic, but tests for privilege. Candidates living in rentals may lack this specific exposure while possessing superior logic.

Manager: Sarah Goal: Service Focus

"Describe a time you received 5-star service at fine-dining."

Click to reveal the trap

The Trap: Socioeconomic Background

Sarah wants service intuition, but tests for background. Elite skills aren't exclusive to luxury dining; don't filter out experts who haven't had the privilege of the venue.

Interactive Workshop

The Interpretation Gap

"Tell me about a time you showed scrappiness."

What do you think a candidate might interpret this as?

Candidate Assumptions:
  • • "When did you work 80 hours?"
  • • "When did you break the rules?"
  • • "When did you have no budget?"
The Accuracy Fix:

"I hear you mentioning hours, but I'm looking for problem-solving. Walk me through the constraints you faced."

Competency Brake Pedal

Rely on Facts

Competencies force the CEO brain to take over. Evaluating measurable skill over vibes.

  • Curiosity: Seeking the root cause before acting.

  • Communication: Articulating complex ideas clearly.

  • Drives Results: Focus on outcomes and persistence.

  • Collaborates: Elevating the work of those around them.

The Evidence Rule

Every rating requires factual behavior observed or a direct quote.

No factual proof = don't give the score.
