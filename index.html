The Danger Zone

The Gut Trap

Recognizing the 10 common "Hiring Shortcuts" that distract us from high performance.

The Art Critic

Trusts their "gut" in the first 10 minutes. Usually just Affinity Bias masquerading as expertise. You're hiring a mirror, not a talent.

The Sponge

Lets everyone interview but has no coordination. They just "soak up" a vibe, resulting in zero actionable evidence.

The Chatterbox

The "Seller." They spend the whole call selling Gravity, hiring the candidate because they were a good listener.

The Prosecutor

Actively looking for flaws only. Trying to "break" the candidate instead of evaluating their actual score on competencies.

The Fortune Teller

Focuses on "What would you do if...?" (Hypotheticals). People give great answers to imaginary problems.

The Animal Lover

Hiring based on a "feeling" of hunger or grit without specific behavioral proof or direct past evidence.

The Armchair Psych

Trying to read "body language" or looking for hidden psychological meanings instead of checking their track record.

The Aptitude Tester

Only cares if they can do the technical job today, ignoring soft skills, curiosity, or long-term contribution potential.

The Genealogist

Hiring the employer brand ("They worked at Google!") instead of evaluating the person sitting in front of you.

The Inquisitor

Asking weird brain-teasers or "gotcha" riddles that have nothing to do with the actual work required for the role.

The Brain Bus

Your Decision Dashboard

The Amygdala

"The Alarm System"

It scans for people flagging as "not like us." It sees Different and flags it as Danger.

How this blocks you: You feel "tight" or "uncomfortable." You write it off as a bad vibe, but it's just your brain resisting difference.

The Hippocampus

"The Pattern Library"

It stores societal tropes and associations. It tells you: "Successful people usually act like THIS."

How this blocks you: You start looking for evidence to prove your first 30-second association right, ignoring all other data.

Prefrontal Cortex

"The Pilot"

The only part that can read a Scorecard. It uses logic to override the "Gut." It's high-energy and gets tired easily.

How this fuels you: This is where objectivity lives. We use Competencies to keep the Pilot in control.

Debrief Room Traps

Recognizing "Brain Noise" in real Gravity huddles.

"The Mesh Fallacy"

"I don't think they'd mesh well with the team."

The Real Issue: Code for Affinity Bias. We want Culture Add, not just similarity.

"The Passion Trap"

"They didn't seem excited about our mission."

The Real Issue: Rewarding extroversion over preparation or actual commitment.

"The Decision For Them"

"They've managed before, they'll get bored here."

The Real Issue: Predicting motivation rather than capability. Let them decide.

"The Pedigree Shortcut"

"They worked at [Big Bank], they won't be scrappy."

The Real Issue: Judging the past employer logo instead of the candidate's grit.

Success Attributes

Gravity's Soft Skill Focus

We focus on observable outcomes and competencies, not "vibes."

  • Curiosity: Do they ask "why"? Do they seek root causes before acting?

  • Communication: Can they articulate complex ideas clearly to different audiences?

  • Drives Results: Do they focus on outcomes and persistence rather than just activity?

The Purpose

Competencies are the tool we use to hack our own biology. They are the specific "Brake Pedal" for the brain.

Neutralize the "Vibe": Measurement of evidence over gut feelings.

Bypass Barriers: We stop asking about pedigree and start asking about ability.
